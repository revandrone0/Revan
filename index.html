Questions

Before you reserve

Are these products flying today?

The perception software and dashboard run as a prototype. The reference airframe flies in PX4 and Gazebo simulation, and the RAVEN airframe is not yet flight-validated. Each product card shows its stage so you know what you are reserving.

Does the AI confirm that a person needs rescue?

No. A detection is a lead for a human to review. RAVEN is decision support for responders and does not fly itself or confirm survivors.

Do I need permission to fly in India?

Drone Rules 2021 and the Digital Sky platform apply to most drones. The rules depend on weight, use and airspace zone, so check the current requirements for your drone class before flying. We include a checklist with every airframe.

Are the specs final?

Figures marked as targets are design goals. They are confirmed after testing and may change before dispatch.

Can I buy only the software?

Yes. The dashboard and the crowd-counting module run with a phone camera or an ESP32 camera node and need no airframe.
